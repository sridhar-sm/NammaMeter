**Effective Date:** February 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

NammaMeter ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

**Key Privacy Principle:** NammaMeter operates entirely offline and stores all data locally on your device. We do not transmit, sell, or share your data with third parties.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Location Data

**What We Collect:**
- GPS coordinates during active trips
- Trip route points (latitude, longitude, accuracy, timestamp)
- Current device location (when permission is granted)

**How We Use It:**
- Calculate distance traveled for accurate fare computation
- Display real-time map view during trips
- Provide route visualization and playback capability
- Enable background location tracking for continuous trip recording

**Storage:**
- Stored locally on your device in the app's secure container
- Associated with trip records
- Retained as long as you keep the trip history
- Deleted when you delete individual trips or clear app data

**iOS Permission:**
The app requests "Location" permission via iOS settings with the prompt:
> "We use your location to measure distance and replay routes."

You can modify location permissions at any time in iOS Settings > NammaMeter > Location.

### 2.2 Trip Data

**What We Store:**
- Fare amount (calculated)
- Distance traveled (kilometers)
- Trip duration (seconds and elapsed time)
- Start and end times
- Waiting time duration
- Trip status (For-Hire, In Progress, Complete)
- City/location configuration used
- Weather/trip conditions (clear sky, surge pricing, night mode)

**How We Use It:**
- Create trip history records
- Calculate driver earnings
- Provide trip details and receipts
- Enable trip search and filtering
- Display trip statistics and summaries

**Storage:**
- Stored as JSON data files locally on device
- Location: App container's Documents directory
- Format: Structured JSON with complete trip information
- Retained indefinitely until manually deleted

### 2.3 Configuration & Preferences

**What We Store:**
- Theme preference (Light, Dark, or System)
- Meter display style selection (Super Mechanical, Super Electronic, Golden Eagle, Bright Digital)
- Meter render mode (Full or Display Only)
- Digit wheel animation style (Disk or Drum)
- City-specific fare rates:
  - Base fare amount
  - Per-kilometer rate
  - Per-minute rate
  - Minimum fare
  - Night time surcharge multiplier
  - Night time window (hours)
  - Waiting charge policy (free minutes, interval, charge amount)
- Selected active city/location

**How We Use It:**
- Customize app appearance and functionality
- Calculate accurate fares based on local rates
- Remember your preferences across sessions
- Enable city switching for different locations

**Storage:**
- Stored in iOS UserDefaults (local device storage)
- Format: Key-value pairs, plain text
- Retained across app sessions until deleted

### 2.4 What We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect:
- ✗ Personal identifying information (name, email, phone)
- ✗ User account or authentication data
- ✗ Payment information or financial data
- ✗ Browsing history or analytics
- ✗ Device identifiers (UDID, IDFA)
- ✗ Crash reports or diagnostics (optional iOS crash reporting only)
- ✗ User behavior analytics
- ✗ Advertisement identifiers
- ✗ Contact information
- ✗ Photo library or media access
- ✗ Calendar or reminder data
- ✗ Health information

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Storage Security

**Local Device Storage:**
- All app data is stored in the app's secure container
- Protected by iOS app sandbox (no other apps can access)
- Protected by iOS file encryption at the system level
- Device-locked when screen is locked

**No Network Transmission:**
- App operates completely offline
- No internet connection required
- No data transmitted to servers
- No cloud storage or sync

**No Encryption Implementation:**
- We rely on iOS system-level protection
- Device password/biometric protection secures access
- No additional encryption layer needed for offline data

### 4.2 Data Deletion

**How to Delete Your Data:**

**Delete Individual Trips:**
1. Open NammaMeter
2. Go to "Trips" tab
3. Swipe left on a trip
4. Tap delete (or use the History Action menu)

**Delete All App Data:**
1. Go to iOS Settings
2. Select NammaMeter
3. Tap "Offload App" (keeps app) or "Delete App" (removes everything)
4. Reinstall if using "Offload"

**Delete Specific Settings:**
- Offload and reinstall the app to reset all preferences
- Or modify settings individually through the Settings tab

**Data Deletion Timeline:**
- Deleted data is immediately removed from device storage
- No recovery possible after deletion
- No backup or archive is maintained

The app uses automated calculations for:

**These are purely computational**, not profiling or decision-making about you as a person.

## 15. FAQ - Common Privacy Questions

### Q: Is my location data secure?
**A:** Yes. Location data is stored locally on your device, protected by iOS app sandbox and device encryption. It's never sent to any server.

### Q: Can you access my trip data?
**A:** No. We have no access to any of your data. All data is stored locally on your device. We cannot view, access, or retrieve it.

### Q: Does the app track me?
**A:** No. Location tracking only occurs during active trips and only when you explicitly start a trip. The app has no background tracking capability beyond what you see.

### Q: What if I lose my phone?
**A:** Your trip data is deleted when you erase your device. If you have iCloud backup enabled, the data may be in your backup. You can disable backup in iOS Settings.

### Q: Can I export my data?
**A:** Trip data is visible in the app. You can manually record trip information. There is no built-in export function, but we can add one if requested via GitHub.

### Q: Does the app use analytics?
**A:** No. We do not use Google Analytics, Fabric, Firebase, or any analytics service. We do not track usage patterns or user behavior.

### Q: Is there advertising?
**A:** No. NammaMeter contains no advertisements, no ad networks, and no ad tracking.

### Q: Does the app collect payment information?
**A:** No. The app is not a payment processor. You do not pay for the app or its features. No payment information is collected.

### Q: How is my data synced to other devices?
**A:** It's not. Trip data is local to each device. Settings do not sync. If you want to use NammaMeter on multiple devices, you must configure each separately.

### Q: What if I want to use a privacy VPN with this app?
**A:** Since the app doesn't use the internet, a VPN won't affect it. The app works completely offline.

### Q: Is this app open source?
**A:** The code is available on GitHub. You can review the code, audit the implementation, and verify the privacy claims made in this policy.
