NAMMAMETER PRIVACY POLICY (SHORT VERSION)
==========================================

Effective Date: February 8, 2026

PRIVACY SUMMARY
===============

NammaMeter is an offline auto rickshaw meter app that:
• Stores ALL data locally on your device
• Does NOT send data to servers
• Does NOT share data with third parties
• Does NOT collect personal information
• Does NOT use analytics or tracking

DATA COLLECTION
===============

We collect ONLY:

1. LOCATION DATA (during trips)
   - GPS coordinates
   - Route points
   - Used for: Distance calculation and map display
   - Storage: Local device only
   - Control: iOS permission settings

2. TRIP DATA
   - Fare amount
   - Distance traveled
   - Trip duration
   - Waiting time
   - Storage: Local device only
   - Control: Delete anytime in app

3. SETTINGS & PREFERENCES
   - Theme choice (Light/Dark)
   - Meter style selection
   - City fare rates (your configuration)
   - Storage: Local device only
   - Control: Change anytime in app

We do NOT collect:
✗ Personal information (name, email, phone)
✗ Payment data
✗ User accounts or login info
✗ Analytics or usage tracking
✗ Device identifiers
✗ Advertising data

LOCATION PERMISSION
===================

Why: To measure distance for accurate fare calculation
When: Only during active trips
Control: iOS Settings > NammaMeter > Location
You can: Allow Always, Allow While Using, or Never

DATA SECURITY
=============

• All data stored locally on your device
• Protected by iOS app sandbox
• Protected by device encryption
• NOT synced to cloud
• NOT sent to any server

DATA DELETION
=============

Delete Individual Trip:
1. Open NammaMeter
2. Go to Trips tab
3. Swipe left and delete

Delete All App Data:
1. iOS Settings > NammaMeter > "Offload App" or "Delete App"
2. Data is permanently deleted

YOUR RIGHTS
===========

You have the right to:
• Know what data is collected
• Access all your data (visible in app)
• Delete any trip or all data
• Control permissions
• Stop using the app anytime

THIRD PARTIES

We do NOT use:
✗ Google Analytics

✗ Any ad networks
✗ Any tracking services
