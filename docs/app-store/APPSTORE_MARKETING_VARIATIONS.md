## Extended Description (Full 4000 characters)

```
NammaMeter is the professional-grade auto rickshaw meter application for iOS, designed for auto rickshaw drivers, auto-rickshaw operators, ride-sharing professionals, and passengers who demand accuracy and transparency.

PROFESSIONAL METER DESIGNS
Choose from four authentic meter styles, each crafted to match traditional auto rickshaw meters:
• Super Mechanical: The classic mechanical meter with traditional drum-style digit wheels
• Super Electronic: Advanced electronic meter with night mode indicator and multiple display fields
• Golden Eagle: Premium aesthetic design with elegant styling
• Bright Digital: Modern LCD-style display for maximum clarity

ACCURATE FARE CALCULATION
NammaMeter uses GPS-based distance measurement and precise time tracking to calculate fares according to your configured rates. Features include:
• Real-time fare calculation as you drive
• Support for unlimited cities with independent rate configurations
• Automatic night time fare multipliers (typically 22:00-05:00)
• Configurable waiting time charges with free wait periods
• Minimum fare protection to prevent undercharging

INTELLIGENT LOCATION TRACKING
Built-in GPS functionality provides:
• Real-time map display showing your current route
• Continuous location tracking with accurate distance measurement
• Route replay capability for trip verification
• Background location updates during trips
• High-precision GPS integration for accuracy

COMPREHENSIVE TRIP MANAGEMENT
• Start and stop trips with a single tap
• Pause trips to switch to waiting time mode
• Complete trip history with timestamps and full details
• Search and filter past trips
• Trip statistics and summaries

COMPLETE CUSTOMIZATION
Configure NammaMeter for any city worldwide:
• Base fare amount
• Per-kilometer rate
• Per-minute time charge
• Included kilometers (if applicable)
• Waiting time policy with free wait minutes and interval charges
• Night time surcharge multiplier
• Night time window (start and end hours)

DISPLAY OPTIONS
Personalize how your meter appears:
• Full view: Shows complete meter interface
• Display only: Shows meter face without controls
• Digit wheel styles: Choose between disk and drum designs
• Light and dark themes for different lighting conditions
• System theme sync or manual selection

OFFLINE FUNCTIONALITY
NammaMeter works completely offline. All calculations happen on your device without requiring internet connectivity. Your trip data is stored securely on your iPhone or iPad.

PERFECT FOR:
✓ Taxi drivers managing earnings and fleet
✓ Auto-rickshaw operators in India and worldwide
✓ Ride-sharing professionals
✓ Fleet managers tracking operations
✓ Passengers verifying fares and understanding charges
✓ Regulatory compliance and dispute resolution

PRIVACY & SECURITY
Your data is yours alone. NammaMeter uses location information only to calculate distances for fare computation. No unnecessary data collection, no cloud syncing of personal trips, and no advertising or tracking.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
• Requires iOS 17.6 or later
• Compatible with iPhone and iPad
• Available in English and Kannada
• Full background location support
• Optimized battery usage
• Native SwiftUI interface

Whether you're a professional auto rickshaw driver ensuring accurate earnings, an operator managing a fleet, or a passenger wanting transparency and fairness in pricing, NammaMeter is your complete solution for auto rickshaw fare calculation.

Download NammaMeter today and experience the confidence that comes with accurate, transparent fare calculations on every trip.
```

## Regulatory & Legal Considerations

### Disclaimers
```
NammaMeter calculates fares based on configured rates and GPS tracking. Fare
calculations are estimates and may differ from actual meter readings due to GPS
accuracy variations or meter calibration differences. Always verify fares with
official transport authorities for your location.
```

### Privacy Policy Requirements
Must include information about:
- Location data usage
- Data storage location (device local storage)
- What data is collected (none for cloud)
- User rights regarding data

### Terms of Use
Consider including:
- Calculation accuracy disclaimer
- Liability limitations
- User responsibilities for rate configuration
- Dispute resolution procedures
