DESCRIPTION (paste into App Store Connect):
================================================================================

NammaMeter is your trusted digital auto rickshaw meter for accurate fare calculation on every ride. Whether you're a auto rickshaw driver managing your fleet or a passenger verifying fares, NammaMeter provides real-time calculations with transparency and precision.

KEY FEATURES:

🚕 FOUR PROFESSIONAL METER STYLES
Choose your preferred meter display:
• Super Mechanical - Classic mechanical meter design
• Super Electronic - Advanced meter with night mode
• Golden Eagle - Premium aesthetic design
• Bright Digital - Modern LCD-style display

💰 ACCURATE FARE CALCULATION
• Real-time fare calculation based on distance and time
• Fare rules engine with active rule highlighting
• Browse fare rules before your trip in the For Hire state
• Itemised fare breakdown on trip completion
• Support for multiple cities with custom rates
• Automatic night fare multipliers
• Waiting time charges with customizable intervals
• Minimum fare protection

📍 INTELLIGENT LOCATION TRACKING
• GPS-based distance measurement
• Real-time map view of your route
• Continuous tracking with background location updates
• Location replay for trip verification

⏱️ TRIP MANAGEMENT
• One-tap trip start and stop
• Pause trips with waiting mode
• Complete trip history with analytics

🎨 CUSTOMIZABLE EXPERIENCE
• Multiple meter display modes
• Digit wheel style options
• Light and dark mode themes
• Portrait and landscape orientation support
• City-specific fare configuration

⚙️ MULTI-CITY SUPPORT
• Base fare amounts
• Per-kilometer rates
• Per-minute waiting charges
• Night time fare multipliers
• Custom rate schedules

📊 TRIP HISTORY & ANALYTICS
• Complete trip records with timestamps
• Fare, distance, and duration tracking
• Search and filter capabilities

Perfect for: Auto Rickshaw drivers, auto-rickshaw operators, ride-sharing professionals, fleet managers, and passengers.

NammaMeter takes the guesswork out of auto rickshaw fares. Requires iOS 17.6+. No in-app purchases. No advertisements.

REVIEW NOTES (for App Review):
================================================================================

NammaMeter is a professional auto rickshaw meter application for iOS. It provides accurate fare calculations based on configurable rates for different cities.

GETTING STARTED:
1. Launch the app - meter view displays immediately
2. Tap "Start trip" to begin fare calculation
3. Tap "Stop trip" to end and save
4. View history in "Trips" tab
5. Configure city rates in "Settings" tab

KEY FEATURES:
- Offline functionality (no internet required for calculations)
- GPS-based distance tracking
- City-specific fare configuration
- Night mode automatic surcharge
- Trip history and analytics
